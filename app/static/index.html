How it works

Plain retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) searches once and hopes the right passage is in the results. ActLens is agentic: the model decides what to search, reads, searches again with new words if needed, and only then answers.

  1. Official text, split by structureThe regulation is parsed into 800 passages: article paragraphs, single definitions, annex parts and recitals, each with its exact citation label.
  2. Hybrid searchKeyword search (BM25) and meaning search (static embeddings) run in parallel and are merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. A search takes under a millisecond on a CPU.
  3. Agent loop with toolsDeepSeek calls search_law and read as tools, up to 6 rounds, and writes the answer with a citation after each claim.
  4. Citation checkEvery citation is matched against the passages the agent actually retrieved. Anything it did not read is flagged, which catches invented article numbers.

Compare the search methods

Free and instant: no AI model involved. Keywords catch exact terms, embeddings catch meaning, and the hybrid merges both. Measured results are on the evaluation page.
